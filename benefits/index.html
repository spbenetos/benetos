Your cards, budgets and renewals, together.

Six parts that answer one question: where did this month's money go, and what is coming next.

  • Today

    See the month at a glance

    Month-to-date spending against your budget, income against what went out, and the renewals coming up next.

  • Cards

    Every card at a glance

    Add a card from a screenshot: Benefits finds it, straightens it and paints over the number, expiry and security code, right on your iPhone.

  • Budget

    Budgets that make sense

    A ring for each category shows what is spent and what is left, with suggested budgets from your last three months and alerts at 80% and 100%.

  • Reports

    Where it all went

    Monthly charts by category, card or merchant, with pace and projection for the month, day-by-day bars and a 12-month trend.

  • Subscriptions

    No surprise renewals

    Monthly and yearly totals, a reminder the day before each renewal or free-trial end, and charges logged for you on the day.

  • Logging

    Log it in seconds

    From the Lock Screen, Control Center, the Action button, Back Tap, a widget or Siri, without opening the app.

Privacy

Private by design.

No account, no ads and no tracking. Benefits has no servers of its own, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

  • Stays on your iPhoneEvery card, purchase and budget lives on your device.
  • Your own iCloudTurn on sync and it is kept in your private iCloud, which only you can read.
  • Locked with Face IDLock the app, and keep your numbers hidden in the app switcher.
  • Yours to takeExport a spreadsheet or a full backup whenever you like.
